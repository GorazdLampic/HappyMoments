## Kids Age-Based Hints

**CURRENT:** One-size-fits-all hint for born after 2010: "That's a big number — ask them how many zeroes it has!"
**PROBLEM:** Charming for 5-year-old, embarrassing for 12-year-old.

| Age | Hint | Why |
|-----|------|-----|
| 3-5 | "Can you count that high? Try together!" | Counting is THE game at this age |
| 6-8 | "How many days of school is that?" | School days = their unit of time |
| 9-12 | *(no hint)* | Scale comparisons need dynamic content, too complex |
| 13+ | *(no hint)* | Teens cringe at anything "for kids" |

**RECOMMENDATION:** Implement only for ages 3-8 (two brackets). Remove for 9+. If too complex for now, remove the feature entirely — a mismatched hint costs more trust than it adds.
