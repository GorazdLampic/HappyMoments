HappyMoments Engine

How milestones are generated, scored, filtered, and displayed — the full pipeline

1. The Pipeline (5 stages)

Every time the app renders milestones for a person, it runs this pipeline:

1
Generate Numbers
specialNumbers.js builds a master set of all "interesting" numbers based on user settings (patterns on/off). Cached after first run.
2
Find Milestones
milestoneCalculator.js iterates 7 time units × all special numbers, keeps those within the time window (default 365 days).
3
Coherence Filter
Inside findAllUpcomingMilestones: removes numbers that don't make sense for a unit (e.g., palindrome 12321 in seconds — too noisy).
4
Score & Sort
roundnessScore() × 0.5 + proximity × 0.5. Cosmic milestones get fixed scores. Result: ordered by "interestingness".
5
Dedup & Display
filterNearbyMilestones removes lesser numbers too close to "very special" ones. Top N shown per column.

2. Number Categories (what's in the master set)

The engine generates these number families. Each can be toggled via DEFAULT_SETTINGS.patterns.

Powers of 10 + Round Numbers ON
Examples: 10, 100, 1000, 500, 2500, 75000, 1000000000
87 round numbers hardcoded in ROUND_NUMBERS array, from 500 to 100,000,000,000. Includes half/quarter points.
Repdigits ON
Examples: 11, 222, 3333, 55555, 7777777
All single-digit repeats from 2 to 11 digits. Digits 1-9 × lengths 2-11 = ~90 numbers. High roundness scores.
Palindromes ON
Examples: 121, 12321, 1234321, 7654567, 1010101
SELECTIVE — not all palindromes. Handpicked ~120 numbers: mountain patterns, binary-style, prime-digit, round, date-like. Max 9 digits.
Sequential ON
Examples: 123, 12345, 987, 98765, 345, 5678
Ascending (123...9), descending (987...1), plus partial sequences of 3-4 digits. ~40 numbers total.
Alternating ON
Examples: 1212, 3737, 252525, 19191919
Two-digit alternating patterns, lengths 4 to 10 (even only). 9×9×4 = ~300 numbers. Only 8+ digit ones pass seconds/minutes filter.
Powers of 2 ON
Examples: 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024, ... 230
23 to 240 generated, but classified only up to 1024 (210). Above 1024, they enter the set but get no "2n" label — just generic "Special number".
Fibonacci ON DEFAULT OFF
Examples: 5, 8, 13, 21, 34, 55, 89, 144, 233, 610, 2584, 46368
Generated up to 100B. Disabled by default in settings (fibonacci: false) but the generation code sets fibonacci !== false, so actually ON. Score bonus: only +8. Most users won't recognize 2584 or 46368.
Scientific Constants ON (Pi, e, φ)
Examples: 314, 3141, 31415, 2718, 1618, 299792458
6 constants defined, 3 enabled by default (Pi, e, Golden Ratio). Each has 5-8 truncation levels. Score bonus: +12.
Asian Auspicious ALWAYS ON
Examples: 88, 168, 520, 888, 1314, 5201314, 8888
~28 hardcoded numbers. Always included regardless of settings. Includes Chinese prosperity (8s), longevity (9s), love codes (520, 1314), Indian sacred (108, 786).
Sacred / Cultural ALWAYS ON
Examples: 3, 7, 12, 33, 40, 108, 786, 1008
Classified inside _classifyAsianLucky() as "sacred" type. Hindu (108, 1008), Islamic (786), Christian (7, 12, 33, 40). Score: +15 as asian_lucky.
Lucky Digits OFF
Default digits [3,7]: 33, 37, 73, 77, 333, 337, 373, ...
All numbers composed only of chosen lucky digits, up to 10 digits. Capped at 3 lucky digits to prevent combinatorial explosion. Disabled by default.
Cosmic / Planetary Returns ALWAYS ON
Examples: 1st Saturn return (~29.5y), Jupiter return (~11.86y), Mars return (~1.88y)
7 bodies: Moon (27.3d), Mercury (88d), Venus (225d), Mars (687d), Jupiter (4333d), Saturn (10759d), Chiron (18520d). Fast planets: only round returns (500th Moon, 100th Mercury). Slow planets: every return.
Total numbers in master set: Roughly ~2,000-3,000 depending on settings. After per-unit coherence filtering, typically 50-200 candidates survive per person per render.

4. Coherence Filter (Stage 3 detail)

Not every number makes sense in every unit. Inside findAllUpcomingMilestones(), the engine applies per-unit gates:

Seconds & Minutes: Only round thousands (n%1000===0), powers of 10, repdigits, alternating 8+ digits, or Asian lucky codes (888, 8888, 1314, etc.) pass. Everything else is rejected.
Hours: Only round hundreds (n%100===0), powers of 10, or repdigits pass when n>100.
Weeks: Only round hundreds (n%100===0) or powers of 10 pass when n>100.
Months: Only round 50s (n%50===0), powers of 10, or cultural numbers (108, 786, 888, 1008, 1314) pass when n>12.
Days: Passes most patterns through, but filters numbers 1001-1005, 2001-2005 etc. (trivially close to round thousands).
Years: Everything passes (range is small, 0-150).

5b. Final display score (in renderPersonColumns)

finalScore = roundnessScore(value) × 0.5 + proximityScore × 0.5

Where proximityScore = max(0, 100 − daysAway × 0.27)
So: today = 100, 30 days = 92, 180 days = 51, 365 days = 1.5

5c. Hero milestone score (findHeroMilestone)

heroScore = rScore × 0.6 + pScore × 0.4

rScore components:
• Birthday: 40 base
• Big milestone (next power of 10): +80
• Very special number: +30
• Saturn return: +60  |  Jupiter/Chiron: +20  |  Other cosmic: +5

pScore:
• ≤30 days: 100 − days×1.5
• 30-365 days: decays 55 → 0
• >365 days: 0
• Birthday within 14 days: +50 bonus

6. Proximity Deduplication (Stage 5)

filterNearbyMilestones() removes "lesser" milestones that sit too close to a "very special" one in the same unit.

What counts as "very special"?

isVerySpecialNumber(num) returns true for:
• num ≥ 1,000,000 (anything a million+)
• Round thousands (num%1000 === 0)
• Powers of 10 ≥ 1000
• Large repdigits ≥ 10,000
• Key Asian: 888, 8888, 9999, 1314, 5201314
• Small special: 2, 3, 5, 10, 50

Filtering threshold

• specialValue ≥ 10,000: filter within 1.5% of value
• specialValue ≥ 1,000: filter within 2% of value
• smaller: filter within ±50

7. Combined / Group Milestones

When 2+ people are in a group, the engine computes their age sum in each unit and finds when that sum hits a special number.

Key insight: If N people are in the group, their combined age increases by N per unit of time (N seconds per second, N days per day, etc.).

unitsNeeded = (targetNumber − currentSum) / N
milestoneDate = now + unitsNeeded × unitMultiplier

The max reasonable value per unit is multiplied by N, so a 4-person family can reach 4× the normal range.

Combined milestones use the same master number set but with no per-unit coherence filter (all special numbers in all units). This means more results but also more noise.

9. What I See as Gaps / Issues

A. Fibonacci is ON despite settings saying OFF
DEFAULT_SETTINGS has fibonacci: false, but generateAllSpecialNumbers() checks settings.patterns.fibonacci !== false, which is false !== false = false — wait, actually this IS correct and Fibonacci IS off. But the check at line 496 uses !== false which means it would be ON if the value were undefined (a new user who never set it). Ambiguous default behavior.
B. Powers of 2 classified only up to 1024
Numbers 2048, 4096, 8192, 16384, 32768, 65536 are IN the master set but classifyNumber() only labels them as "2^n" if num ≤ 1024. Above that, they get "Special number" — the user doesn't know why 4096 is there. Should extend classification or remove them from the set.
C. No "double digit" round numbers in days (e.g., 11111 days)
Repdigits are generated and classified, but the days coherence filter doesn't explicitly handle them. They DO pass through (only 1001-1005 near round K are blocked), but their roundnessScore is modest (+20 + digits×3 = 35 for 11111). A 5-digit repdigit in days could get outranked by a generic round thousand.
D. No "nice fractions" concept
The engine doesn't recognize numbers like 12345 days (sequential!), 11111 hours (repdigit!) because these get filtered by the hours coherence rule (must be round hundreds for hours >100). A 5-digit repdigit in hours would be rejected.
E. Weeks are heavily filtered — almost nothing shows
Only round hundreds and powers of 10 pass for weeks >100. That means the only week milestones you'll ever see are 100, 200, 300... 1000, 1500, 2000, 2500, 3000, 5000 weeks. No palindromes, no repdigits, no Fibonacci in weeks. A 48-year-old has ~2500 weeks — they'll see very few upcoming week milestones.
F. Scoring doesn't reward multi-type numbers
roundnessScore() checks each pattern independently, but a number that is BOTH a palindrome AND a repdigit (like 11111) gets both bonuses additively. However, a number that is a Fibonacci number AND a power of 2 (like 8) only gets +8+8=+16. There's no multiplicative bonus for multi-dimensional specialness.
G. Potentially missing: milestone number ranges for 25-45 year olds in DAYS
A 30-year-old is at ~10,957 days. The interesting day milestones in the next year are 11,000 and 11,111 (repdigit). But 11,111 is a repdigit that roundnessScore gives only 35 points. Meanwhile 11,000 gets 20+45+30=95 points. The app might never surface 11,111 ahead of the round number — but 11,111 is arguably more fun and shareable than "11,000 days".
H. No "binary milestone" category
Numbers like 10000 in binary (=16), 100000 (=32), 10000000000 (=1024) are already covered as powers of 2, but the engine doesn't flag "your age in days is exactly a binary round number" as a distinct milestone type. Binary/CS people would love "214 = 16,384 days old" but the label only shows "Special number" above 1024.

10. What's Working Well

Coherence filter is smart
Per-unit gating prevents the "10,392nd second" nonsense. Only meaningful numbers show for each time scale.
Proximity dedup prevents clutter
filterNearbyMilestones() removes 10,001 when 10,000 is present. Good UX.
Cosmic milestones are properly deprioritized
After the v50-52 fixes: max 1 cosmic globally, never first in list, low fixed scores (5 for Mercury, 20 for Jupiter, 60 for Saturn).
Combined milestone math is correct
The "sum increases by N" formula is accurate. Calendar-accurate months/years with overflow handling.
Asian/sacred numbers are always on
Smart decision for global market — 888, 520, 108 resonate deeply in target demographics.

Summary: The Engine's Philosophy

  • Generate broadly — ~2,000-3,000 candidate numbers from 11 categories
  • Filter per unit — coherence rules reduce to ~50-200 candidates per person
  • Score = 50% impressiveness + 50% proximity — balance "wow factor" with "happening soon"
  • Deduplicate — remove lesser numbers near very special ones
  • Display top N — typically 7 per person, expandable

The system is well-engineered but has room for improvement in: (1) repdigit/palindrome scoring relative to round numbers, (2) week milestones being too aggressively filtered, (3) powers of 2 classification above 1024, and (4) potentially missing number families that are "nice" but don't fit current categories.
