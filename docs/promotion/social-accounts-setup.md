## Content Strategy: Animations for Social Sharing

### What works best for social (ranked):

1. **Screen recording of the app** — Most authentic, easiest to create
   - Record yourself entering a birthday on phone
   - Capture the counter animation moment
   - Capture the milestone reveal
   - Add text overlay: "I just found out I turn 1 billion seconds old on..."
   - Tools: Phone screen recorder (built-in), CapCut for editing

2. **Milestone card images** — Already built into the app
   - Generate via app's "Share as Image" feature (1080x1080 square or 1080x1920 story)
   - Post directly to Instagram feed/stories
   - Add to carousel posts

3. **Animated number counter** — High impact
   - Record the landing page (happymoments.app/landing.html) billion result
   - The scrolling from 500M → 750M → 1B → 1.5B → 2B is visually satisfying
   - Good for Reels/TikTok (15-30 seconds)

4. **"Before/After" format** — Works on all platforms
   - Before: "I didn't know when my billion was"
   - After: Screen recording of the result
   - Add reaction face

### Video scripts for first week:

**Script 1 — Discovery (15 sec, Instagram Reel):**
```
[Face to camera]
"Did you know you have a billionth-second birthday?"
[Cut to phone screen showing app]
"Mine is [date]."
[Cut back to face]
"When's yours? Link in bio."
```

**Script 2 — Family (30 sec, TikTok):**
```
[Text overlay: "I checked my mom's birthday"]
[Screen recording: entering mom's date]
[Result appears: "Mom turns 2 billion seconds on..."]
[Text overlay: "Now I have a reason to call her"]
[CTA: happymoments.app]
```

**Script 3 — Challenge (15 sec, Instagram Story):**
```
[Milestone card with your number]
[Text: "I'm [X] days old today"]
[Poll sticker: "Do you know YOUR number?"]
[Swipe up/link: happymoments.app]
```

### Content creation workflow (Sunday batch, 30 min):
1. Open happymoments.app on phone
2. Generate 3-5 milestone cards for different people/dates
3. Take 2-3 screen recordings of the counter animation
4. Use CapCut/InShot to add text overlays
5. Schedule posts for the week (Instagram: Creator Studio / Meta Business Suite)

## Influencer Outreach (Phase 2-3, after 50+ users)

### Target categories (ethical approach — give tool, let them discover):

**Tier 1 — Numerology & Math Educators (10-50K followers)**
- Numerology Instagram accounts
- Math-is-fun educators on TikTok
- "Today in history" accounts
- Birthday/zodiac content creators

**Tier 2 — Parenting & Family (50-200K followers)**
- New parent bloggers
- Family milestone trackers
- "Mom life" content creators
- Grandparent-focused accounts

**Tier 3 — Greeting card & gifting (any size)**
- Custom card shops
- Gift recommendation accounts
- Party planning influencers

### Outreach template (DM):
```
Hi [name]! 👋

I built a free app that finds hidden number milestones in people's lives — like when you turn exactly 1 billion seconds old, or 10,000 days.

I think your audience would love discovering their own milestones. Would you be open to trying it? happymoments.app

No strings attached — just thought it might make for fun content. Happy to offer premium access if you'd like.

Gorazd
```

### Outreach template (Slovenian, for SL influencers):
```
Živjo [ime]! 👋

Naredil sem brezplačno aplikacijo, ki najde skrite številčne mejnike v življenju — kot je tvoj "milijardni rojstni dan" (kdaj si star/a točno 1 milijardo sekund).

Mislim, da bi tvoji sledilci uživali v odkrivanju svojih mejnikov. Bi probala? happymoments.app

Brez obveznosti — samo mislil sem da bi bil zanimiv content. Gorazd
```

### Do NOT:
- Pre-research their families or birthdays (feels invasive)
- Offer money (feels inauthentic for this kind of product)
- Mass-DM (send 3-5 per week, personalized)

### DO:
- Mention something specific about their content
- Give premium access for free
- Offer to create custom milestone cards for their content
- Be patient — response rate will be 10-20%
