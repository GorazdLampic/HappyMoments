## One-Page Description (200 words)

HappyMoments is a Progressive Web App that reveals the hidden poetry of numbers in your life. Enter any birthday, anniversary, or meaningful date, and the app calculates dozens of fascinating milestones you never knew existed — across seconds, minutes, hours, days, weeks, months, and years.

The app finds round numbers (10,000 days old), palindromes (12,321 hours), Fibonacci sequences, mathematical constants (Pi, Golden Ratio, Euler's number), and culturally significant numbers from traditions around the world: Chinese lucky numbers (888, 520, 1314), Indian sacred numbers (108, 786, 1008), and more.

A unique Team feature lets users add family members and friends to discover combined milestones — when a couple's combined age reaches 50,000 days, or when a family's total is exactly 100,000 days.

Built by physicist and entrepreneur Gorazd Lampic as a personal project using AI pair programming, the app shipped in one week with support for 21 languages from day one. It runs entirely in the browser (or as an Android app), stores all data locally on the user's device, uses no tracking cookies, and requires no account to use.

Free with an optional EUR 1.49/year premium tier for unlimited people and watermark-free image cards.

## Feature List

### Core Features
- Milestone detection across 7 time units (seconds, minutes, hours, days, weeks, months, years)
- Round number milestones (1,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 / 100,000 / 1,000,000 / 1,000,000,000)
- Palindrome milestones (12,321 days, 54,345 hours, etc.)
- Repdigit milestones (1,111 / 2,222 / 3,333 / 11,111 / 22,222 etc.)
- Fibonacci number milestones
- Mathematical constant milestones (Pi, Golden Ratio, Euler's number, speed of light)
- Quick Find: enter any number and see when you'll reach it

### Cultural Number Systems
- Chinese lucky numbers: 888 (triple fortune), 520 (love / "wo ai ni"), 1314 (forever / "yi sheng yi shi"), 168 (prosperity / "yi lu fa"), 88 (double fortune)
- Indian sacred numbers: 108 (sacred completeness), 786 (Bismillah), 1008 (auspicious)
- Universal cultural numbers: 7 (luck), 13 (superstition), 42 (meaning of life)

### Team Milestones
- Add multiple people (family, friends, colleagues)
- Combined age milestones ("Together you are 100,000 days old")
- Event sets (My Family, Friends, Work)
- Multi-person selection with toggle buttons

### Sharing
- WhatsApp, Viber, email sharing with pre-written messages
- Beautiful image cards in two formats: square (1080x1080) and Instagram Story (1080x1920)
- Shareable deep links

### Technical
- Progressive Web App — works in any modern browser, installable on Android/iOS
- Offline support via service worker
- Local data storage (privacy-first, no server-side user data)
- Export/import data as JSON backup
- 21 language support with automatic detection

### Premium (EUR 1.49/year)
- Unlimited people and groups
- Unlimited Team tab access
- Watermark-free image cards
- No promotional banners

## Founder Bio

**Gorazd Lampic**
Physicist, entrepreneur, inventor.

Gorazd Lampic is the founder and CEO of Quantum Wave Ltd (Kvantni Val d.o.o.), a Slovenian deep-tech company developing quantum-nature technologies and advanced semiconductors. Previously, he co-founded Elaphe Propulsion Technologies in 2006 and served as CEO for 18 years, building the company from 2 founders to 150+ employees and establishing it as the world leader in in-wheel electric motors.

He holds an MSc in Astrophysics from the University of Ljubljana (research on Active Galactic Nuclei using Hubble Space Telescope data), has 10+ personal patents and managed a 30+ patent family portfolio, and has won 20+ EU-funded projects totaling over EUR 10M in grants.

He currently serves as Chairman of EPoSS (Transportation working group), Board Member of EGVIA, and Member of EU Commissioner Zaharieva's 50-Entrepreneur Expert Group advising on European startup and scale-up policy.

HappyMoments is his first consumer product — built as a personal project to explore AI-assisted development and consumer product dynamics.
