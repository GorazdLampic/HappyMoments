# HappyMoments — Viral Launch Strategy v2

*Rebuilt from scratch based on founder corrections, 27 May 2026*
*Previous version (v1) focused on "When's Your Billion?" as the core value. That was wrong.*

---

## What Changed and Why

The v1 strategy made three fundamental errors:

1. **It treated a feature as the value proposition.** "When's your billion?" is a hook for ~30-year-olds. It is not the reason the app exists.
2. **It centred on self-discovery.** The real power is not "I found something about ME." It is "I have a reason to reach out to YOU."
3. **It ignored the content pipeline.** Viral loops on paper are worthless without a systematic, daily engine that puts content in front of people.

This v2 strategy is built on the founder's three core insights:

> "The billion is just for people around 30 years old. Others have other hooks."
>
> "It is about finding the reason to celebrate. Making happy moments. Having the reason to celebrate."
>
> "It is about empathy. The ability to have content for a ping to a friend, to an ex-girlfriend, to a family member. To get back in touch. To strengthen relationships."

## 1. THE REAL VALUE PROPOSITION

### What the app is NOT

It is not a calculator. It is not a number trivia tool. It is not "When's Your Billion?" (that is one hook among dozens).

### What the app IS

**HappyMoments is a relationship tool disguised as a number calculator.**

It gives you a reason to text someone today. Not a generic "hey, how are you?" but a specific, personal, surprising reason: "Did you know our friendship just turned 5,000 days? That happened last Tuesday."

The numbers are the excuse. The celebration is the point. The reconnection is the real value.

### The value proposition (one sentence)

> **"Never miss a reason to celebrate with the people you love."**

### Supporting statements (for different contexts)

| Context | Statement |
|---------|-----------|
| For the app store | "Discover hidden reasons to celebrate with your people." |
| For a friend | "It gives you a personal reason to text someone today." |
| For social media | "The app that turns ordinary days into celebrations." |
| For parents | "Know when your child hits 5,000 days, 1 million minutes, and 50 other milestones you'd otherwise miss." |
| For couples | "520 days together means 'I love you' in Chinese. When's yours?" |
| For reconnectors | "Find a reason to reach out to someone you've been meaning to text." |
| For cultural communities | "Every culture has sacred and lucky numbers. The app knows when you hit them." |

### Why this reframe matters

The v1 framing ("I found something about MYSELF") creates one share. One moment of surprise, one share, done. The user has no reason to return.

The v2 framing ("I have a reason to reach out to SOMEONE") creates a habit loop. Every time the app surfaces a milestone for someone you care about, you have a reason to act. That act strengthens a relationship. That emotional reward makes you check again.

The retention problem that all three v1 analysis agents identified ("party trick app, open once, never return") is solved not by features but by reframing. You don't return because of push notifications. You return because the app told you your mother turns 25,000 days old next week, and that is a reason to call her.

## 2. THE HOOKS BY DEMOGRAPHIC

Every age group has their own "wow" moment. The billion-seconds hook only lands for people aged 27-35. Here is the full map:

### 18-25: The Social Generation

**Their hooks:**
- "You just turned 8,000 days old and didn't even know it"
- "Your best friend's 10,000th day is coming up — throw a party"
- Fibonacci milestones (nerdy, shareable in Gen Z culture)
- Combined milestone with their friend group ("Squad turns 100,000 days together")
- Billion seconds is coming (future anticipation)

**Why they share:** Social currency. Being the person who discovers cool things before others. Group dynamics. Challenges.

**Key channels:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, WhatsApp group chats, Snapchat

**Content format:** 15-second reveals, "did you know" hooks, group challenges, duet-able content

**Emotional driver:** Belonging, being interesting, group identity

### 25-35: Relationships and Milestones

**Their hooks:**
- Billion seconds (the original hook, ~31.7 years, lands perfectly here)
- Relationship milestones: "520 days together = 'I love you' in Chinese"

- "Your relationship just passed [round number] hours"
- First baby milestones: "Your baby is 1,000 days old"

**Why they share:** Romantic gestures, relationship identity, milestone-celebrating culture (this generation posts everything)

**Key channels:** Instagram Stories, WhatsApp (personal messages to partner/friends), Pinterest (milestone party ideas)

**Content format:** Beautiful cards, couple milestone reveals, "send this to your person" prompts

**Emotional driver:** Love, partnership celebration, sharing life moments

### 35-50: Parents and Reconnectors

**Their hooks:**
- "Your daughter turns 5,000 days old next Tuesday" (parental milestone)

## 5. THE SOCIAL PROOF FLYWHEEL

This is how each user action creates content that attracts more users, without any manual intervention.

### 5.1 The Flywheel Mechanics

```
User discovers milestone
    |
    v
Shares with friend (message includes app link + deep link)
    |
    v
Friend sees THEIR milestones instantly (deep link pre-fill)
    |
    v
Friend enters OWN birthday --> discovers OWN milestones
    |
    v
Friend shares with THEIR friends (link included again)
    |
    v
[REPEAT]
```

**Every share carries the virus.** The share message always contains the app link. The image card always contains the HappyMoments watermark (free tier). The deep link always leads to instant value.

### 5.2 Built-In Viral Elements

| Element | How It Spreads | Already Built? |
|---------|---------------|---------------|
| Share message with app link | Every WhatsApp/Viber/email share includes happymoments.app | YES |
| Image card watermark | Every free-tier card has "happymoments.app" | YES |
| Deep-link pre-fill | Clicking a shared link shows the sender's milestones instantly | YES |
| "Challenge Your Group" | One tap shares to a WhatsApp group of 20-50 people | YES |
| "When's YOURS?" prompt | Every share message ends with a question that invites response | YES |
| Daily history facts | Shareable content even for people who don't use the app | YES (calendar built) |

### 5.3 The Challenge Chain

When User A challenges a WhatsApp group, 3-5 people respond. Each of those people may challenge THEIR groups. This creates branching chains:

```
User A --> Group (30 people) --> 4 install --> 2 challenge THEIR groups
    Each new group (25 people) --> 3 install --> 1 challenges another group
        ...
```

Even with modest conversion (10% install from group, 20% of installers challenge another group), each seed user generates 1.5-2.5 additional users over 3 "generations."

### 5.4 The Content Flywheel

Daily social media posts create a secondary flywheel:

```
Daily post (nice number + historical event)
    |
    v
Followers see --> some share (especially "Send this to..." format)
    |
    v
Shared content reaches non-followers
    |
    v
Curiosity --> visit happymoments.app
    |
    v
Discover personal milestones --> share THOSE
    |
    v
[Merge into main viral loop]
```

The daily content is NOT about the app. It is about interesting numbers. The app is the tool that makes it personal.

### 5.5 User Testimonials as Fuel

Once real users exist, their stories become marketing material:

- "I called my university friend because the app told me our friendship is 10,000 days old. We hadn't spoken in 3 years."
- "We threw a party for my daughter's 5,000th day. She loved it."
- "I sent my wife a card on our 520th day together. She had no idea what 520 meant in Chinese. Now she does."

**Collection method:** After a user shares a milestone, show a gentle prompt: "Did this make someone smile? Tell us (20 words)." Use the best responses (with permission) in future marketing.

## 7. THE ONBOARDING (Rewritten)

### v1 Onboarding: Self-Focused

```
Welcome → Enter YOUR birthday → See YOUR milestones → (maybe share)
```

Problem: The first action is about the user. The share is an afterthought.

### v2 Onboarding: Relationship-Focused

```
Welcome: "Who do you want to celebrate?"
→ Option A: "Someone I care about" → Enter THEIR name + birthday
→ Option B: "Me first" → Enter own birthday (traditional flow)

If Option A:
→ Show THEIR milestones → "Here's what's coming up for [name]"
→ Prompt: "Send [name] their next milestone"
→ THEN: "Now add yourself. See what you have in common."
```

**Why this works:**
1. The very first action is a GIFT (discovering someone else's milestone)
2. The first share is emotionally driven (sending a surprise to someone)
3. The user adds themselves SECOND — to compare and discover combined milestones
4. This creates two "aha" moments: one for the friend's milestones, one for their own

**Critical detail:** Option A must be the default/prominent option. Option B ("Me first") should be available but secondary. Most people will choose "me first" anyway, and that is fine. But those who choose Option A will have the highest-quality first experience and the highest share rate.

### Onboarding for Deep-Link Recipients

When someone clicks a shared link:

```
See FRIEND'S milestones instantly (zero friction)
→ "When's YOUR special number?"
→ Enter own birthday → See YOUR milestones
→ "You and [friend] together: [combined milestone]!"
→ Prompt to share, add more people
```

This is already built. It is the most powerful onboarding path.

### First-Session Checklist

For maximum activation, the first session should achieve:
1. Enter at least one person's birthday (theirs or someone else's) -- YES if they completed onboarding
2. See at least one "wow" milestone -- YES (hero card)
3. Take ONE social action (share, send, or challenge) -- this is the gap to optimize

The gap is #3. Currently ~10-20% of users share in their first session. Target: >30%. The v2 onboarding (relationship-first) and the "Send this to [name]" prompt directly after milestone display should increase this.

## 8. THE WEEKLY/MONTHLY RHYTHM

What keeps the app relevant over time? Not push notifications alone (those are reminders). The app needs to provide ongoing VALUE that makes people want to check it.

### Daily

**In-app:** "Today's Number" — a historical event with a nice number. This changes every day and is interesting even without any personal milestones. It is ambient content that rewards opening the app.

**Social media:** One post using the day's nice number (see content pipeline, Section 3).

**Push notification (if upcoming milestones exist):** "[Name] turns [milestone] in 3 days!" — only when there is something real to celebrate.

### Weekly

| Day | What | Channel |
|-----|------|---------|
| Monday | "Milestone of the Week" — spotlight one upcoming milestone type | Social media + in-app banner |
| Wednesday | Mid-week check: "This week's milestones for your people" | Push notification (if applicable) |
| Friday | "Weekend milestones" — what's happening Saturday/Sunday | Push notification + social post |
| Sunday | "Next week preview" — milestones coming in the next 7 days | In-app notification |

### Monthly

- **"Month in Numbers" summary:** "This month, 3 milestones happened for your people. Here's the next month."
- **New historical events added:** Keep the Daily Nice Numbers calendar fresh
- **Seasonal angle:** Tie to holidays, seasons, cultural events

### Quarterly / Seasonal

| Period | Theme | Content |
|--------|-------|---------|
| New Year (Jan) | "Your Year in Numbers" | Spotify Wrapped equivalent: all milestones in the coming year, shareable card |
| Valentine's (Feb) | "Love Numbers" | 520, 1314, combined couple milestones, "days we've been in love" |
| Spring | "New Beginnings" | Baby milestones, new relationships, fresh starts |
| Summer | "Family Milestones" | Kids' summer birthdays, family trips as milestone markers |
| Back to School (Sep) | "Friendship Numbers" | School friendships in days, teacher milestones |
| Holidays (Nov-Dec) | "Year-End Celebration" | "Your Year in Numbers" card (the big annual feature), gift store push |

### The "Year in Numbers" Card (Annual Feature)

This is the single most important retention and viral feature for the future. Like Spotify Wrapped, it creates:

1. **FOMO:** Everyone's sharing theirs, you want yours too
2. **Social currency:** Your numbers are unique, interesting, shareable
3. **Annual habit:** Users return every December/January
4. **Data richness:** The more people you've added, the richer your card

**Card contents:**
- Total milestones that happened this year for all your people
- The most interesting milestone of the year
- Combined milestones for your groups
- Cultural milestones hit this year
- Preview: the biggest milestone coming next year
- "You celebrated with [X] people this year"

**Implementation timing:** Build for December 2026/January 2027. Announce early ("Your Year in Numbers is coming in December — add your people now to get the full picture").

## 9. THE MEASUREMENT FRAMEWORK

### 9.1 Funnel Metrics (AARRR)

**Awareness → Acquisition → Activation → Retention → Revenue → Referral**

| Stage | Metric | Target (Month 1) | Target (Month 3) | Source |
|-------|--------|------------------|------------------|--------|
| **Awareness** | Social media impressions/week | 5,000 | 25,000 | Platform analytics |
| **Awareness** | Play Store listing views/week | 100 | 500 | Play Console |
| **Acquisition** | Installs/week | 20 | 100 | Play Console + Analytics |
| **Acquisition** | Onboard completion rate | >60% | >70% | onboard_complete / page_view |
| **Activation** | First share in first session | >15% | >30% | share events / onboard_complete |
| **Activation** | Added 2+ people in first session | >30% | >50% | event_added count |
| **Retention** | D1 (next day return) | >20% | >30% | Analytics |
| **Retention** | D7 (7-day return) | >10% | >20% | Analytics |
| **Retention** | D30 (30-day return) | >5% | >12% | Analytics |
| **Retention** | Push notification open rate | >15% | >25% | Firebase |
| **Revenue** | Premium conversion (of D30 users) | >3% | >8% | Stripe |
| **Revenue** | Gift store orders/month | 0 | 5 | Printful |
| **Referral** | K-factor (shares x conversion) | >0.10 | >0.25 | Calculated |
| **Referral** | Deep-link opens per share | >0.3 | >0.5 | Analytics |
| **Referral** | Challenge group conversion | >5% | >10% | group_challenge events |

### 9.2 Content Metrics

| Metric | What It Tells You | Check Frequency |
|--------|------------------|----------------|
| Best-performing platform | Where to double down | Weekly |
| Best-performing content format | What to create more of | Weekly |
| Share rate per post | Which posts drive referrals | Per post |
| "Send this to" engagement | Whether relationship framing works | Weekly |
| Influencer outreach response rate | Whether approach is working | Monthly |
| Daily Nice Number engagement | Whether ambient content has value | Weekly |

### 9.3 North Star Metric

**"Celebrations triggered per week"**

This is defined as: number of shares + challenge sends + gift orders + deep-link opens per week.

Not installs. Not revenue. Celebrations triggered. If people are celebrating with their people through HappyMoments, everything else follows: retention (they come back for the next milestone), revenue (they want premium/gifts for celebrations), referral (every celebration involves another person who sees the app).

### 9.4 Anti-Metrics (What NOT to Optimise For)

| Anti-Metric | Why NOT to Optimise for It |
|-------------|--------------------------|
| Raw installs | Installs without activation are vanity. 100 active users > 1,000 dead installs. |
| Time in app | This is a quick-action app. Users should get in, discover, share, get out. Long sessions = confusion. |
| DAU | Most users will open weekly or monthly, not daily. That is fine. Force-fitting daily habits is wrong for this product. |
| Premium conversion of new users | New users should not be pitched premium. Let them love the free tier first. Convert at D30+. |

## 12. RISK REGISTER

| Nobody shares (viral loop fails) | Medium | Critical | Test multiple hooks per demographic. If no hook works after 100 users, the product needs fundamental change. |
