# HappyMoments — Friend & Family Rollout Plan

## The Key Insight

You DON'T need to know their birthdays. **The app asks them to enter their own.** Your job is just to get them excited enough to open the link.

The best invitation is personal: "I found out something cool about YOUR birthday" — but you can only say this if you know their date. For others, use curiosity: "When's your billion?"

---

## Tester Tiers

### Tier 1: Inner Circle (5-10 people)
**Who:** Family, closest friends, partner, kids, parents
**You know their birthdays:** YES
**What to do:**

1. Before sending the app, enter THEIR birthday yourself
2. Screenshot their coolest upcoming milestone
3. Send them a PERSONAL message with the screenshot:

> "Hey [name]! Did you know you'll be exactly 1 billion seconds old on [date]? That's in [X] days! I built an app that finds these — check it out: [Play Store link]"

or

> "[Name], you're turning exactly 15,000 days old next month! I made something that calculates all these hidden milestones. Would love your feedback: [link]"

**Ask them to:**
- Install and add their own birthday
- Add at least one other person (partner, child)
- Try the Team tab
- Tell you what's confusing or broken
- Click "This made me happy!" if they like it

---

### Tier 2: Good Friends (10-20 people)
**Who:** Close colleagues, good friends, neighbours
**You know their birthdays:** SOME — maybe month, not exact date
**What to do:**

For those whose birthday you know (even approximately):
> "Hey! I'm testing a new app. I checked — you're about to hit an amazing number milestone. Install and see what it is: [link]"

For those whose birthday you DON'T know:
> "I built something fun — it tells you when you turn 1 billion seconds old, 10,000 days, and other crazy milestones. Would you try it and give me honest feedback? [link]"

**Ask them to:**
- Give honest feedback (what's confusing? what's cool?)
- Share one milestone with someone (tests the viral loop)
- Rate: would you use this monthly? Why/why not?

---

### Tier 3: Extended Network (20-50 people)
**Who:** Acquaintances, LinkedIn contacts, extended family, hobby groups
**You know their birthdays:** NO
**What to do:**

Use the CURIOSITY hook — no personalization needed:
> "When did you turn 1 billion seconds old? Or when WILL you? I made an app that finds hidden numerical milestones in your life. Free, no registration needed: [link]"

or post in a group chat / WhatsApp group:
> "Fun experiment — enter your birthday and see what comes up. Some of the numbers are wild. happymoments.app (or Play Store link)"

**Ask them to:**
- Try it for 2 minutes
- Share if they find something interesting
- No pressure for feedback

---

### Tier 4: Public Soft Launch (50-100)
**Who:** Social media followers, broader WhatsApp groups, community groups
**You know their birthdays:** NO
**What to do:**

Post on your social media:
> "I've been working on something — an app that finds the poetry in numbers. When's YOUR billion? Try it free: [link] #WhenIsYourBillion"

Or a more personal angle:
> "I discovered my daughter turns exactly 5,000 days old next Tuesday. What are YOUR hidden milestones? [link]"

---

## Rollout Timeline

| Week | Tier | People | Goal |
|------|------|--------|------|
| Week 1 | Tier 1 (inner circle) | 5-10 | Find critical bugs, validate core experience |
| Week 1-2 | Tier 2 (good friends) | 10-20 | Test viral sharing, get honest feedback |
| Week 2-3 | Tier 3 (network) | 20-50 | Test curiosity-based acquisition, measure retention |
| Week 3-4 | Tier 4 (public) | 50-100 | Test organic sharing, measure viral coefficient |

## What to Track

After each tier, check your admin dashboard (happymoments.app/admin.html):

| Metric | What it tells you | Target |
|--------|------------------|--------|
| Total page_view events | How many opened the app | — |
| onboard_complete events | How many entered a birthday | >60% of page views |
| event_added events | How many added 2+ people | >40% of onboards |
| tab_switched to "combined" | How many explored Team | >30% |
| auth_signed_in | How many created accounts | >15% |
| happy_click events | How many enjoyed it | >20% |
| Shares (check share events) | Viral coefficient | >0.3 shares per user |
| checkout_started | Premium interest | Track, don't expect much yet |

## Feedback Questions to Ask (after they've used it)

1. What was the FIRST thing you noticed or felt?
2. Was anything confusing? Where did you hesitate?
3. What's the coolest milestone you found?
4. Did you share anything? If not, why not?
5. Would you check this app again? Weekly? Monthly?
6. What's missing that you'd want?
7. Would you pay €1.49/year for this? What would make you pay?

## Preparing Your Contact List

You don't need a spreadsheet of birthdays. Instead:

| Column | What | Why |
|--------|------|-----|
| Name | Friend's name | To personalize the message |
| Tier | 1/2/3/4 | When to contact them |
| Know Birthday? | Y/N/Approximate | Determines message type |
| Platform | WhatsApp/Email/SMS/Instagram | How to reach them |
| Tech Level | High/Medium/Low | Expect more/less feedback |
| Market | SI/EU/Asia/US | Tests different locales |
| Sent | Date sent | Track who you've contacted |
| Response | Installed?/Feedback? | Track results |

Start filling this in — even 30 names is enough for a solid beta.
