Version 2.0 · June 7, 2026 · Status: DRAFT — organic progression: individual → combined → group
Based on Gorazd's edits to onboarding-steps.md. Critical review included.

Core insight: The group EMERGES from adding people. You don't name a group upfront — you add people, see the magic of combined milestones, THEN "finalize" the group by naming it. This is the right order.

Critical Review

What works well:
  • Organic progression: Me → one person → combined → name the group → add more. The group concept emerges naturally.
  • Share appears at the right moment (Screen 5: you just discovered something about someone you care about).
  • Group naming after value (Screen 6: you've already seen the combined milestone — now naming feels earned).
  • Linear path through screens 1-3: every user builds personal investment before adding others.
  • Low friction first add: Screen 4 asks for just ONE person, not a group.
Potential issues to discuss:
  • Screen 5 is dense: Hero reveal + milestone list + share + continue. On small screens, the "continue" button may be below the fold. Consider: keep the hero reveal above fold, milestones below, buttons sticky at bottom.
  • Screen 7 → 8 transition: After adding 2 people on Screen 7, do we show each person's individual milestones? Or just the group combined? Gorazd's edit says "show their milestones" then "show combined" — this could be split into Screen 8a (individuals) and 8b (combined) or merged.
  • Second group flow: "Create another group" on Screen 8 restarts at Screen 6 (name + combined) → 7 (add members) → 8 (reveal). The second-group "Me" needs to be auto-added to the new set.
  • 8 screens is a lot. Most users will reach Screen 5 or 6. The fast path (skip on Screen 4) is 4 screens. The full path is 8. This is acceptable if each screen delivers value — and they do.
Recommendation: Implement exactly this 8-screen flow. Screen 5 merges the old "their reveal" + "their milestones" into one screen (saves a tap). Screen 6 is the key innovation — combined milestone + group naming on one screen. Screen 7 reuses the group-builder pattern. Screen 8 is the exit with share + optional second group.
